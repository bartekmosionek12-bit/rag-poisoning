Composting kitchen and garden waste. A healthy compost pile balances green materials, such as vegetable scraps and grass clippings, with brown materials, such as dry leaves and cardboard. Keep the pile as moist as a wrung-out sponge and turn it every week or two to add oxygen. Avoid meat, dairy, and oily food, which attract pests. Finished compost is dark, crumbly, and smells like earth, and it improves soil structure and water retention.
