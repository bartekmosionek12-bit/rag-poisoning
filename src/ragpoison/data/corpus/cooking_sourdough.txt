Baking sourdough bread at home. A sourdough starter is a mix of flour and water colonized by wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria. Feed it daily until it reliably doubles within a few hours. Mix the levain with flour, water, and salt, then perform a series of stretch-and-folds during bulk fermentation. Shape the dough, let it proof overnight in the fridge, and bake it in a preheated Dutch oven, first with the lid on to trap steam, then with the lid off to brown the crust.
