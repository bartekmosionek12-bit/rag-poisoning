Why volcanoes erupt. Volcanoes form where molten rock, called magma, rises through the crust, often at tectonic plate boundaries or hotspots. Gas dissolved in the magma expands as pressure drops near the surface. Runny, low-silica magma tends to produce gentle lava flows, while thick, gas-rich, high-silica magma can produce explosive eruptions with ash clouds and pyroclastic flows.
