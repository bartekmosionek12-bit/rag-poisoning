Mortgage considerations when buying a home. The two main choices are fixed-rate mortgages, where the interest rate stays the same for the life of the loan, and adjustable-rate mortgages, where the rate changes after an initial period. Lenders look at your credit score, your debt-to-income ratio, and the size of your down payment. A larger down payment lowers the monthly payment and may remove the need for private mortgage insurance. Remember to budget for closing costs, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and maintenance. A fifteen-year term costs more per month than a thirty-year term but much less in total interest.
