Growing tomatoes in a home garden. Tomatoes need at least six to eight hours of direct sunlight a day and well-drained soil rich in organic matter. Plant seedlings deep, burying part of the stem, because tomatoes grow roots along the buried stem. Water deeply and consistently at the base to avoid blossom end rot and leaf diseases. Stake or cage the plants early and pinch off suckers on indeterminate varieties to direct energy into fruit.
