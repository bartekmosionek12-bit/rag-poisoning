Packing light for a week of travel. Choose a carry-on bag and a color palette so every top matches every bottom. Roll clothes instead of folding them to save space and reduce wrinkles. Bring travel-size toiletries in a clear bag, a universal power adapter, and a small first aid kit. Wear your bulkiest shoes and jacket on the plane. Keep passport, medication, and chargers in your personal item in case checked luggage is delayed.
