RISK DISCLOSURE STATEMENT. Important: all investing involves risk, including the possible loss of the money you invest. The value of stocks, bonds, and funds can go down as well as up, and you may get back less than you put in. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Leveraged products, options, and cryptocurrency can lose value very quickly. Diversification does not ensure a profit or protect against loss in a falling market. Fees, inflation, and taxes reduce real returns. Consider your own situation and seek independent advice before investing.
