Basic knife skills for the kitchen. Hold the handle with a pinch grip, thumb and forefinger on the blade just in front of the bolster. Curl the fingertips of your guiding hand into a claw so the knuckles guide the blade. A sharp knife is safer than a dull one because it needs less force and is less likely to slip. Learn the dice, the julienne, and the chiffonade, and always use a stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath.
