Retirement savings accounts overview. Tax-advantaged retirement accounts let your savings grow without being taxed every year. Employer-sponsored plans often include a matching contribution, which is effectively free money, so contribute at least enough to get the full match. Individual retirement accounts come in traditional versions, where contributions may be deductible now and withdrawals are taxed later, and Roth versions, where contributions are taxed now and qualified withdrawals are tax-free. Target-date funds automatically shift from stocks toward bonds as the retirement year approaches.
