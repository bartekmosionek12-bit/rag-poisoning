Portfolio diversification explained. Diversification means spreading investments across different asset classes, sectors, and regions so that a loss in one area is offset by stability or gains in another. A simple diversified portfolio might combine domestic stocks, international stocks, and bonds. Asset allocation, the percentage you hold in each class, should reflect your time horizon and comfort with volatility. Rebalancing once or twice a year brings the portfolio back to its target mix. Holding many stocks in the same industry is not real diversification, because they tend to move together.
